Child Safety Standards

Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)  ·  Last Updated: September 2026

Zero-Tolerance Policy: Novix Messenger maintains a strict, non-negotiable zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We strictly prohibit any use of our platform to create, possess, facilitate, distribute, or promote material that harms or exploits children.

1. Purpose & Scope

Novix Messenger is committed to creating a safe and secure communication platform. These Child Safety Standards detail our zero-tolerance stance, preventative policies, community enforcement mechanisms, and cooperation with child protection organizations and global law enforcement in compliance with Google Play Child Safety Standards Policy.

2. Strictly Prohibited Content and Conduct

We enforce strict prohibitions against any conduct that compromises the safety, well-being, or innocence of minors:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Uploading, sending, sharing, soliciting, or storing images, video, text, or any depiction of child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Child Grooming & Solicitations: Initiating, engaging in, or facilitating predatory communication or behavior directed at minors.
  • Sextortion & Exploitation: Threatening, coercing, or blackmailing children or young people.
  • Facilitation: Promoting or distributing links, external resources, or groups organized to trade or discuss child abuse material.

3. In-App Reporting & Protective Controls

Novix Messenger empowers every user with real-time in-app reporting and moderation mechanisms:

  • Report User: Any user can tap on another user's profile, select "Report User", choose a violation category, and submit the report directly to our moderation team.
  • Immediate Blocking: Users can block anyone instantly to immediately stop all calls, chats, and interactions.
  • Content Moderation: Reported accounts and media undergo prompt review and action.

5. Enforcement & Legal Referrals

Whenever a violation involving CSAE or CSAM is identified:

  • Immediate Termination: The offending account is permanently terminated.
  • Hardware & Network Bans: Device fingerprints and network identifiers are blacklisted.
  • Content Eradication: All offending files, messages, and records are permanently removed from all systems.
  • Law Enforcement & NCMEC Reporting: In compliance with 18 U.S.C. § 2258A and international child safety standards, we report all instances of CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and relevant international law enforcement agencies.

6. Compliance with Global Child Protection Laws

Novix Messenger complies with all relevant regional, national, and international child protection statutes. We actively cooperate with competent government authorities, judicial bodies, and accredited child welfare organizations to keep the digital world safe for children.
